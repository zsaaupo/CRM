# eManager | Software requirements specification

**Purpose.** eManager is a browser-based operations workspace. Staff receive purchases into a warehouse, sell and dispatch products, reconcile stock and money, and manage employees and payroll. The signed-in dashboard summarizes the user's account. Staff enter each business workflow through permission-controlled navigation.

**Edition 1.2 · 28 November 2026.** Intended readers are product owners, designers, engineers and QA reviewers. FR identifies a functional requirement, BR a business rule, UI a screen family, NFR a quality requirement and AC an acceptance scenario. Use the coverage index in section 6 to find the screens and scenarios for a requirement.

**System boundary.** Django serves the HTML workspace and session-authenticated APIs. Browser scripts handle interactive forms, tables, POS and report requests. The server decides access, validates transactions, calculates posted amounts and generates supported PDF documents. The specification describes repository behavior and sets design targets for responsive layouts, accessibility and error recovery. Figma reference sizes and prototype data are design choices.

## 1. Access and functional scope

“Operator”, “cashier” and “reviewer” below describe jobs. Administrators configure roles and permissions. A person may perform several jobs through one assigned role.

| ID | User task | Required behavior |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 | Sign in and administer access | Support signup, email one-time code verification and resend, then email/password sign-in. Provide own profile and logout. Authorized admins manage users, assign a single role, edit its permission matrix and configure warehouse access. |
| FR-02 | Maintain catalog and contacts | Manage products by SKU, category, brand, type, unit, status, currency and suppliers. Print barcode labels. Track raw materials against warehouse stock and flag quantities at or below their reorder level. Maintain supplier, customer, biller and lead directories. |
| FR-03 | Control stock | Show balances per product and warehouse, stock charts and activity. Save multi-line transfers as drafts. Completion moves stock once between distinct warehouses. Support stock adjustments and full, partial or blind counting workflows. Finalization records counted quantities and variances. An authorized operator can create a variance adjustment. |
| FR-04 | Purchase and return goods | Create, edit or import purchase orders with supplier, warehouse, currency, exchange rate and item lines. Receiving requires an active warehouse and valid positive quantities in the product unit. Receipt adds stock once and triggers the applicable accounting entry. Track purchase returns and refunds against the source order. |
| FR-05 | Quote, sell and collect payment | Issue quotations, record acceptance or decline, and convert eligible accepted quotes to sales. Support sales orders, imports, payments and returns. At POS, find products by name, SKU or barcode, save a draft or complete a sale with one or more tenders. Support coupons, gift cards and printable receipts. |
| FR-06 | Fulfil and deliver | Manage chalans (dispatch documents), packing slips and couriers. Assign a delivery to a chalan, record tracking details and display status history, including failed or returned deliveries. |
| FR-07 | Reconcile finances | Maintain financial accounts, income, expenses and categories. Record fund and money transfers. Review cash flow, ledger, daybook and balance sheet with the relevant account, date and warehouse context. |
| FR-08 | Manage staff and payroll | Maintain employees, departments, schedules, attendance, holidays and leave requests. Configure salary components, compensation, adjustments, deductions and loans. Preview payroll by period and currency, calculate a run, review, approve and record salary payments. Expose payslip detail and payroll reports. |
| FR-09 | Configure and inspect records | Manage trade statuses separately from product statuses. Provide a read-only audit log with timestamp, actor, action and record reference. Generate permission-scoped documents, including purchase, sale and transfer PDFs. |

## 2. Business rules and data relationships

| ID | Governing rule | Implication for records and screens |
|---|---|---|
| BR-01 · FR-01 | Warehouse operations require both the action permission and an applicable warehouse scope. For ordinary users, scope comes from a single role assigned one warehouse or all warehouses. | Limit pickers and lists to that scope. A branch user with transfer read access can inspect transfers involving their branch. Writing a transfer requires access to both endpoints. |
| BR-02 · FR-02 | Each party has a unique normalized code and a name. One party may be both supplier and customer. Biller is an exclusive designation. A raw material extends one catalog product and identifies its warehouse and optional reorder level. | Reuse the same party identity across its eligible directories. Derive raw-material quantity from the product's warehouse balance. A threshold of 5 flags a balance of 5. |
| BR-03 · FR-03 | Each product has one stock balance per warehouse. Transfers require distinct endpoints, positive quantities and sufficient source stock. A count snapshots expected stock. Variance equals counted minus expected quantity. | Show transfer balances changing together on completion. Count finalization saves observations. Posting a linked adjustment changes stock and must reject stale variances if the relevant stock moved since the snapshot. |
| BR-04 · FR-04 | Purchase orders retain their currency and exchange-rate snapshot. Rate means base-currency units per order-currency unit, with a rate of 1 for base currency. Line net equals quantity × unit price less the line discount. Tax applies to line net. | Display order currency with totals. Round purchase line net and tax to four decimal places, half up. Sum net, tax and shipping once. Changing a catalog price later must preserve the recorded purchase price. |
| BR-05 · FR-04, FR-05 | A purchase return references received purchase lines. A sales return references completed sale lines. Cumulative completed return quantities may not exceed the original line quantity. | Show original, previously returned and remaining quantities. Completing a purchase return removes units from the original warehouse. Completing a sales return restores them. Record the applicable refund with the stock update. |
| BR-06 · FR-05 | A quotation moves from Draft to Issued, then Accepted or Declined. Issue and customer response must fall within the applicable validity window. Conversion links an accepted quote to one sales order. Revising an issued quote creates a linked draft and marks the issued version Superseded. | Keep the quotation reference, validity date, status and linked order or revision visible on detail. Offer only valid actions. Opening a converted quote again must lead to the same sales order. |
| BR-07 · FR-06 | A chalan has at most one delivery. Handover requires a generated packing slip and active courier, and deducts stock. Delivery posts the applicable payment. A return after handover restores dispatched stock. | Present the current status, valid next statuses and timestamped history. Courier reassignment is available while Assigned or Cancelled. A failed delivery can be retried, delivered or returned. |
| BR-08 · FR-07, FR-08 | An account ledger covers a selected date range. Closing balance equals opening balance plus inflow less outflow. Payroll approval requires a reviewed run with current inputs. | Keep account, period and currency visible next to financial results. A reviewer can return a reviewed payroll run to calculation. Changed inputs require recalculation before approval. |
| BR-09 · FR-09 | Product statuses and trade statuses have independent code sets. Status codes are immutable after creation. The default draft trade status and trade statuses referenced by orders are protected from deletion. | Show the code read-only on edit. Explain a blocked deletion in context. A trade label on an order is separate from its operational receipt or completion flag. |
| BR-10 · FR-07 | Fund transfers and money transfers have separate registers. Each posting requires two distinct active accounts in scope, matching currencies, a positive amount and sufficient source funds. | One transfer reference links the source outflow and destination inflow. Save both entries together. A retry with the same request key and identical details returns the existing transfer. |
| BR-11 · FR-08 | An employee applies for leave through their linked account. The end date is inclusive. An authorized reviewer may apply on behalf of an employee. Only a pending request can be approved or rejected. Reviewers cannot decide their own leave. | Show approval actions only to an eligible reviewer. Reject approval if the employee already has approved leave on any requested date. On success, retain the deciding user and time. |
| BR-12 · FR-08 | A salary payment attempt belongs to one payslip in an approved or payment-processing run. Available amount equals net pay less succeeded and pending attempts. A failed attempt releases its reserved amount. | Display Pending, Succeeded and Failed as distinct outcomes. A successful resolution records reference and actual payment date. A failed resolution records the reason. The run reaches Paid when every payslip is settled. |

## 3. Frontend specification

**Workspace shell.** Use a fixed 248 px white sidebar with the eManager wordmark, expandable module groups and highlighted current link. Use an 80 px sticky top bar with “Workspace / [page]” on the left and an avatar menu with Profile and Logout on the right. Use initials when an image is unavailable. Give the main content 40 px horizontal padding on desktop. Show only destinations and actions available to the signed-in user.

**Responsive layout.** Design reference frames at 1440, 768 and 390 px wide. Below 960 px, replace the sidebar with a menu button and overlay drawer. Below 640 px, use a 70 px top bar and 18 px content gutters. Stack form groups and POS panels vertically. Keep wide line-item tables horizontally scrollable within their panels. Opening a drawer moves focus inside it. Escape or backdrop dismissal returns focus to the trigger.

**Visual system.** Use Segoe UI with a system sans-serif fallback. Page title 30 px semibold, body and input text 14 px, field labels 13 px semibold. Use white panels, stone borders `#E7E5E4`, dark text `#1C1917` and 16 px panel corners. Primary buttons use a dark fill and white text with emerald hover `#047857`. Standard inputs are at least 46 px high with 12 px corners. Use emerald focus `#059669`, pale green selection `#ECFDF5` and inline error text `#B91C1C`. Pair every status color with a readable label.

| Frame ID | Layout and content, in reading order | Primary interaction |
|---|---|---|
| UI-01 Auth | Center a single card up to 448 px wide. Stack title, labeled fields, inline errors, full-width submit and account links. Login has email, password and visibility toggle. Verification has email, code and resend. | Successful verification offers Sign in. Successful login opens UI-02. |

Example user row has avatar, identity, status and permitted row actions. Place pagination below rows. | Search, open a record or create one. Keep filters visible when a search has zero matches. |
| UI-04 Order editor and detail | Group party, warehouse, dates and currency above repeating product lines. Each line shows product, unit, quantity, price or cost, discount and line total. Follow with shipping, totals, notes and save action. Detail leads with reference and state, followed by the saved lines and totals. | Save a purchase draft, inspect it, then Receive. Detail shows receipt state. Give the configured trade status its own label. |

| UI-07 Review and reporting | Payroll run detail leads with period, currency and status, then employee results and available review actions. Show only transitions valid for its current state and the user's permissions. |

| UI-09 Returns | Start with the eligible source order, its party, warehouse and reference. Use one row per source line with original, returned, remaining and requested quantity. Show the draft reference and completion state on detail. | Select lines, save the draft and complete the return. Attach over-limit errors to the affected line. |
| UI-10 Delivery | Lead with chalan reference and current status. Group courier, destination and tracking number together. If handover requires a packing slip, explain that prerequisite. After success, refresh the status, history and available actions together. |
| UI-11 Leave request | Start with Leave type, Start date, End date and optional Reason. Place “The end date is included” beside the date field. Show the Employee selector only to users who can apply on behalf of someone. On detail, group employee, type, dates and status above the reason. | Submit creates a pending request. An eligible reviewer sees Approve request and Reject request. Once decided, replace the actions with the status, reviewer and review time. |
| UI-12 Financial transfer | Enter from Fund transfers or Money transfers and preserve that register in the title. Group From account and To account together, then Amount with currency, Date and optional Reference. On mobile, place the source directly above the destination. | Submit posts both ledger entries. On a currency mismatch, identify To account. On insufficient funds, identify Amount. Preserve the accounts and date for correction. |
| UI-13 Salary payment | On payroll run detail, show saved payslips with Employee, Gross pay, Deductions, Net pay and Breakdown. Follow with Salary payments. Select payslip, amount and method. Pending attempts have a separate outcome form. | Succeeded reveals Payment reference and Actual payment date. Failed reveals Failure reason. After resolution, refresh the available amount, attempt status, run status and audit history. |

### Screen details for data-heavy tasks

- **Product directory · UI-03.** At `/inventory/products/`, the row identity is the product name. Follow with SKU, type, status, sale price and permitted actions. Keep SKU readable at narrow widths. Use the same product name and SKU in order lines, stock counts and catalog search so staff can match a physical label to its record.
- **Receiving · UI-04.** In the editor, distinguish the editable trade status from the Receive action on the saved record. On detail, place the order reference, supplier and warehouse above the item table. A receiver must be able to verify the unit and quantity before posting. Surface a missing warehouse at the header and a mismatched unit on the affected line.
- **Checkout · UI-05.** At `/sale/pos/`, use a 1.25:1 cart-to-catalog grid with a 20 px gap. Stack panels at widths up to 1000 px. Cart columns are Product, Price, Qty, Discount / unit, Subtotal and Remove. Emphasize Grand total below tax and shipping. In Payment, repeat a tender group for each method. Cash exposes Cash received, card or cheque supports a reference, and gift card exposes the card number and balance check. Keep Back to cart available before completion.
- **Ledger · UI-07.** At `/accounting/ledger/`, group Account, Start date, End date and Generate. Results begin with the selected account and period, then Opening balance, Total inflow, Total outflow and Closing balance. Table columns are Date, Source, Reference, Inflow, Outflow and Balance. A valid empty period displays its balances and an explicit empty-row message.

**Figma components.** Use Auto Layout for the shell, toolbars, form rows, cards and dialog actions. Let content panels fill available width and buttons hug labels. Create reusable sets named `Nav/Item`, `Field/Input`, `Action/Button`, `Status/Badge`, `Data/Table` and `Overlay/Dialog`. Include focused, invalid and disabled field variants, plus idle and saving button variants. Data panels need loading, populated, empty and error states. Empty search results offer a filter reset. Failed saves preserve entered values and identify the field or action requiring attention.

## 4. Interface and quality requirements

| ID | Requirement | Verification point |
|---|---|---|
| NFR-01 · Authorization | HTML pages and APIs enforce access on the server. Session writes require a CSRF token. Self-service verification activates the account and login establishes the session. | Exercise a protected page, its API and an out-of-scope record using a restricted role. The response must protect the record even if the URL is entered directly. |
| NFR-02 · Transaction integrity | Stock deductions, receipts, return postings and their associated financial effects must commit consistently. Repeating a completed operation must preserve posted quantities and amounts. | Submit a receipt twice. Attempt a multi-line transfer with insufficient stock on one line. Verify balances on every affected line and warehouse. |
| NFR-03 · Error recovery | Display field validation beside the input. Use a form-level message for permission, conflict or service errors. After an ambiguous network failure during posting, retrieve the saved state before offering another posting attempt. | A failed submission retains the user's editable values. A conflict identifies the affected record and the recovery action, such as refreshing a delivery or starting a fresh count. |
| NFR-04 · Accessible interaction | Design target: all critical flows support keyboard operation, visible focus and persistent field labels. Announce async outcomes, associate errors with their fields and give icon-only actions accessible names. | Complete a purchase form and dismiss a dialog by keyboard. At 390 px, long references and table overflow must leave page-level controls reachable. |
| NFR-05 · Documents and traceability | PDF generation uses the caller's permissions and warehouse scope. Supported shared documents include purchase orders, sales orders, quotations, transfers and packing slips. Generating a document is a read operation. | Compare document reference, lines and totals with the authorized source record. Downloading a PDF must preserve stock balances and workflow state. |

## 5. Acceptance scenarios

| ID | Given / when | Observable result |
|---|---|---|
| AC-01 · FR-01 | A visitor opens a protected page. A signed-in user opens an administration page without its required permission. | The visitor goes to login. The signed-in user receives a 403. Session-based writes require a valid CSRF token. |
| AC-02 · FR-03 | An operator has one warehouse assigned. An all-warehouse operator completes a transfer of 3 units from A (10) to B (2). | The first operator's stock list contains only their warehouse. The transfer draft leaves balances at 10 and 2. Completion yields 7 and 5. Repeating completion leaves those balances intact. |
| AC-03 · FR-04 | A receiver opens a saved order for 5 units with 2 on hand, then submits Receive twice. | On-hand becomes 7. The order shows received, with a single receipt effect on stock and applicable payment posting. |
| AC-04 · FR-05 | A cashier scans an item, takes payment and completes the transaction successfully. | The receipt identifies the sale and total. Recent transactions includes the sale, and stock reflects the sold quantity once. |
| AC-05 · FR-08 | Payroll inputs change after calculation and a reviewer attempts to approve the reviewed run. | Approval is blocked and the reviewer is directed to recalculate using current inputs. |
| AC-06 · UI-03–06 | At 390 px, a keyboard user opens and closes navigation, edits a quantity and submits an invalid value. | Focus returns to the menu trigger after dismissal. The input error is associated with the quantity field. Other entries remain available and the table scrolls within its panel. |
| AC-07 · FR-02 | A party with code SUP-01 exists. An operator attempts to add another with the same code in lowercase. | Validation identifies the code collision. The directory retains one record for that code. |
| AC-08 · FR-04, FR-05 | Run this check for a received purchase and a completed sale. Each source line has 8 units, and completed returns already account for 3. An operator requests 6 more. | Each line shows 5 remaining and rejects 6. Stock and refund postings retain their previous values. |
| AC-09 · FR-06 | An assigned delivery has an active courier and sufficient stock. The dispatcher attempts handover before generating the packing slip, then generates it and retries. | First attempt leaves the delivery Assigned and stock unchanged. The successful attempt marks it Handed over, deducts the dispatched units once and adds a history entry. |
| AC-10 · FR-07 | For the selected account and period, opening balance is 100, inflow is 75 and outflow is 20 in the same currency. | The ledger shows closing balance 155. The dated rows and their running balances reconcile to that figure. |
| AC-11 · FR-09 | An authorized administrator edits a trade status label whose code also exists in product statuses. | Order screens use the new trade label. Product screens retain the original product-status label. |
| AC-12 · FR-03 | A blind count snapshots 12 units and the operator records 10. The relevant stock moves before the linked adjustment is posted. | Blind entry conceals the expected quantity. Review shows a variance of −2. The stale adjustment is blocked and identifies the SKU requiring a fresh count. |
| AC-13 · FR-07 | Two eligible accounts in the same currency hold 100 and 20. Post a fund transfer of 30, then repeat the submission with the same request key. | Balances are 70 and 50. Each ledger has one entry for the transfer reference. The fund transfer register contains one transfer. |
| AC-14 · FR-08 | An employee has approved leave on 10–12 May. A reviewer attempts to approve a pending request for the same employee covering 12–13 May. | The shared date triggers an overlap error. The second request stays Pending. The reviewer can still reject it. |
| AC-15 · FR-08 | A payslip has net pay of 100, a successful payment of 40 and a pending attempt of 35. An operator requests another 30. | Only 25 is available, so the request is blocked. Resolving the pending 35 as Failed with a reason raises the available amount to 60 and preserves the failed attempt in history. |
